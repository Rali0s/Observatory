# The Observatory identity

The public platform is now named **The Observatory**. Its new horizontal logo combines an open book, observatory dome and guiding star with a serif wordmark. Forest green and parchment remain the brand colors.

Asset: `../web_platform/static/observatory-logo.png`. Generated with the built-in image generation tool and installed in the Django header. Previous logo assets are retained as history.

## Generation prompt

Create one refined horizontal logo lockup for a literary writing, reading and narrative-analysis platform named The Observatory. Exact wordmark text: 'The Observatory'. Elegant highly legible editorial serif typography. To its left, a simple distinctive emblem combining an open book with an observatory dome and a small guiding star. No NT letters. Flat forest green #254D3C and warm parchment #F4EEDF. Transparent background. Wide horizontal composition about 3:1 with emblem left and the name on the right, name large, restrained spacing, minimal outer margins. Sophisticated literary press identity, clean vector-like shapes, calm and timeless. No tagline, mockup, shadows, texture, gradients, 3D or additional text. This is a finished website masthead logo, not a presentation board.
