# Novel Tools brand

Novel Tools is the publishing community and free toolkit. The Observatory is the analysis workspace. Reading Room is public discovery and reading.

- Parchment #F4EEDF, ivory #FFFCF5, forest #254D3C, sage #E3E9DC, ink #252B25.
- Serif headings and reading text; sans-serif controls; generous reading width and stacked mobile panels.
- Original geometric NT diamond mark: `web_platform/static/brand-mark.svg`, also the favicon. Preserve proportions and clear space.
- Voice: calm, literary, direct. Analysis provides revision evidence, not a verdict on writing quality.
- Copy: Free tools. A place to publish. Publishing paused. Final renewal window. Redemption required.

Pen names and chosen publication text are public. Login names, manuscripts and analysis are never automatically published. Never imply deletion for nonpayment.
