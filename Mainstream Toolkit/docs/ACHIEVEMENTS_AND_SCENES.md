# Achievements and evolving scenes

The existing banner receives five cumulative illustrated layers: lantern and first light, blossoms, birds, constellations, and illuminated observatory windows. The original image stays intact; overlays and nine custom SVG badges use the beige/green/gold palette. Mobile cropping is aligned between the background and overlay; reduced-motion preferences disable reveal animation.

Five core badges advance the scene once each, in any order: one project; 500 current manuscript words; one saved analysis; three scene cards on a board or three revision notes; 3,000 current manuscript words or a first published post. Every scene is attainable with free tools. Additional community badges reward a first post, three posts, likes from ten distinct readers, and a verified Bitcoin ordinal.

Progress uses only the owner's content. Current chapter text takes precedence over revision snapshots within a project; without chapters only the latest revision's word count counts. Snapshots are not summed. Earned achievements are unique per account and permanent after content removal. Existing activity is evaluated on a private workspace, achievements or signed-in feed visit. New qualifying content is evaluated after the successful write transaction commits.

The Achievements page shows progress, lock states and unlock dates, and lets a writer choose an earned public badge and scene. Public display defaults off; endpoints reject unearned selections. Public profiles never show private counts, notes, manuscript titles or next milestones. Opting in does disclose the selected badge or scene stage. No payment or mint request is triggered by an achievement.

The workspace and signed-in Reading Room show personal progress; anonymous visitors retain the original banner. Profile showcases show the owner's chosen unlocked stage. All unlocks are cosmetic, with no change to payment or publishing authorization. A legacy achievement's timestamp records the first evaluation, not a reconstructed historical date.
